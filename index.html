FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does setup take? +
Around 10 minutes. You'll fill in a 5-step form (your services, prices, schedule, branding) and we generate your bot instantly. Drop in one line of code or share the link — that's it.
Will it sound like a robot? +
No. You set the tone — friendly, formal, casual, professional. Most customers can't tell they're chatting with a bot, and we always offer a clean handover to a human if they ask.
What channels does it work on? +
Web chat, SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, Facebook Messenger, and email. Pro and Enterprise plans include all channels.
Does it integrate with my calendar? +
Yes — Google Calendar, Outlook, and Apple Calendar are supported on Pro and above. Bookings appear in real time and we never double-book.
Can I cancel any time? +
Yes. Monthly plans, cancel from your dashboard with one click. No fees, no questions.
What if I have unusual pricing or services? +
During onboarding you build your own pricing table — per-bedroom, hourly, flat fees, surcharges, anything. The bot only quotes from what you've configured.
