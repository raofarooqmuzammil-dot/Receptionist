Embed Code
Add your AI receptionist to your website in one minute.
🔗 Your unique embed snippet
Paste this single line of code on your website. It loads the chat widget and connects it to your AI receptionist. Anyone visiting your site can then ask questions and book services.
—
How to install
1
Copy the embed code above
Click the Copy button. The snippet is now on your clipboard.
2
Open your website's HTML
If you use WordPress, Squarespace, Webflow, Wix or similar — look for a "custom code" or "header / footer scripts" section in your site settings. Paste the snippet there. If your site is custom-built, paste the snippet right before the closing </body> tag.
3
Save and publish
A small chat bubble will appear at the bottom-right of every page. Visitors click it to start chatting with your AI receptionist.
4
Test it
Open your website in an incognito window. Click the chat bubble. Ask something — the bot should respond using your business info, services, and pricing.
